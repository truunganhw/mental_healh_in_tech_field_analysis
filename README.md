# mental_healh_in_tech_field_analysis

This project uses MySQL to process and clean a survey dataset on the search for mental health treatment. It also analyzes how features such as gender, region, work benefits, and company size influence the high rate of mental health issues.

Based on the analysis, I can conclude the following findings:
- Women tend to experience mental health problems more frequently than men in the technology sector.

- Australia has the highest percentage 61.9% of people needing mental health treatment, while France has the lowest demand at 15.38% in the tech sector.

- Companies with small to medium-sized employees (1 to 500) tend to experience significant work pressure and its impact on mental health, while companies with 500 or more employees have a treatment requirement of only 45-52% over 100%.

Create database, and table inside the SQL workbench, and import data https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/anth7310/mental-health-in-the-tech-industry.
